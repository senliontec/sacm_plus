\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We organize the evaluation around three questions: (i) whether fixed-slot
deployment improves over test-time selection on the same trained model;
(ii) which training components contribute; and (iii) whether the model
transfers to unseen datasets and novel structure classes.
Section~\ref{sec:datasets} describes the protocol;
Sections~\ref{sec:main_results} and~\ref{sec:ablation} report the main
results and the ablation study; Section~\ref{sec:per_dataset} examines
per-dataset behavior; Section~\ref{sec:qualitative} provides qualitative
evidence; and Section~\ref{sec:limitations} discusses limitations.
DCD-specific hyperparameters are given in the supplement.
