\section{Method Details}
\label{sec:supp_method}

This supplement provides the architecture, exact loss definitions, and
training hyperparameters that are summarized in the main paper.

\subsection{Adapter Architecture}
\label{sec:supp_adapters}

The image encoder is a SAM ViT-L with 24 Transformer blocks and token width
$C=1024$. All pretrained encoder parameters are frozen. Adapter-I is inserted
into the residual MLP path of each block and refines the tokens locally:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
a^{I}_l(u)&=W^{up}_l\,\sigma\!\left(W^{down}_l\,\mathrm{LN}(u)\right),\\
u&\leftarrow u+\mathrm{MLP}(\mathrm{LN}(u))+a^{I}_l(u),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where $\sigma$ is GELU and the bottleneck width is $0.1C$. Adapter-E is
applied at each block boundary:
\begin{equation}
e_l=a^{E}_l\!\left(\mathrm{LN}(x_l)\right),
\qquad
x_{l+1}=x_l+e_l .
\end{equation}
The 24 adapter outputs $\{e_l\}_{l=1}^{24}$ are passed to the decoder.
Adapter-F aggregates them into a multi-layer structural descriptor:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
z_l&=W_l\,\mathrm{GAP}(e_l),\\
\alpha&=\mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\mathrm{FFN}([z_1;\dots;z_{24}])\right),\\
f&=\sum_{l=1}^{24}\alpha_l e_l,
\end{aligned}
\qquad
F\leftarrow F+\mathrm{MLP}_{F}(f),
\end{equation}
where $F$ is the upsampled decoder feature. The prompt encoder is frozen; no
sparse prompt is used, and the dense prompt is the learned no-mask embedding.
This adapter design follows SACM~\cite{zhu2026sacm}.

\subsection{Candidate Decoding}
\label{sec:supp_candidates}

The mask decoder contains one IoU token and four mask tokens. A Two-way
Transformer updates these tokens with the image feature and the dense prompt.
Four Stage-1 output MLPs produce coarse masks
\begin{equation}
M_k^{(1)}=\mathrm{MLP}_k^{(1)}(t_k;F),
\qquad k=0,\dots,3,
\end{equation}
where $t_k$ is the $k$-th mask-token descriptor. Stage-1 confidence is the
maximum spatial activation,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
g_k&=\max_{u}M_k^{(1)}(u),\\
w&=\mathrm{softmax}(g),\qquad
s=\mathrm{argsort}(w)~\text{descending}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The descriptors are reordered according to $s$, and four Stage-2 output MLPs
produce the refined masks in ranked order:
\begin{equation}
M_j^{(2)}=\mathrm{MLP}_j^{(2)}\!\left(t_{s_j};F\right),
\qquad j=0,\dots,3.
\end{equation}
Thus slot~0 is the gating champion, and slot~3 is the last-ranked candidate
on every image. The decoder also contains an IoU prediction head for each
candidate. It is not supervised during training and is not used at inference.

\subsection{Training Objective}
\label{sec:supp_losses}

Every Stage-2 head receives the dense mask loss
\begin{equation}
\ell_{\mathrm{dense}}(\hat y,y)
=0.7\,\mathrm{BCE}_{w=2}(\hat y,y)+0.3\,(1-\mathrm{Dice}(\hat y,y)),
\label{eq:supp_dense}
\end{equation}
where BCE uses a foreground weight of 2 and the targets are soft bilinear
masks. Training uses gating selection, so the gating champion (slot~0) is the
anchor and keeps full weight. The remaining three heads form an averaged
auxiliary term with a total weight of $0.25$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{dense}}
=\ell_{\mathrm{dense}}(M_0^{(2)},y)
+\frac{0.25}{3}\sum_{k=1}^{3}\ell_{\mathrm{dense}}(M_k^{(2)},y).
\label{eq:supp_dense_total}
\end{equation}
All four Stage-2 heads also receive soft-clDice~\cite{shit2021cldice} and
Skeleton Recall~\cite{kirchhoff2024skeletonrecall} at $512\times512$
resolution:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{top}}
=\sum_{k=0}^{3}\beta_k
\left(0.1\,\ell_{\mathrm{clDice}}(M_k^{(2)},y)
+0.1\,\ell_{\mathrm{skel}}(M_k^{(2)},y)\right),
\label{eq:supp_top}
\end{equation}
with $\beta_0=1$ and $\beta_{k>0}=0.25/3$. The topology term is linearly
ramped from 0 to 1 between epochs 5 and 10.

Three role losses specialize the auxiliary slots. Slot~1 receives an
additional soft-clDice term; slot~2 receives a boundary-weighted BCE+Dice
loss with pixel weights $1+4B$, where $B$ is a $5\times5$ morphological
boundary band; and slot~3 receives a recall-heavy Tversky loss with
$\alpha=0.3$ and $\beta=0.7$. The role loss is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{role}}
=0.1\!\left(\ell_{\mathrm{clDice}}(M_1^{(2)},y)
+\ell_{\mathrm{bnd}}(M_2^{(2)},y)
+\ell_{\mathrm{tv}}(M_3^{(2)},y)\right),
\label{eq:supp_role}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\ell_{\mathrm{bnd}}(\hat y,y)
&=\mathrm{BCE}_{1+4B}(\hat y,y)+\left(1-\mathrm{Dice}(\hat y,y)\right),\\
\ell_{\mathrm{tv}}(\hat y,y)
&=1-\frac{TP}{TP+0.3\,FP+0.7\,FN}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:supp_bnd_tv}
\end{equation}
A zero-initialized linear fusion of the four Stage-2 masks is trained as an
auxiliary output:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
M_{\mathrm{fus}}={}&M_0^{(2)}+\mathrm{Conv}_{1\times1}\Big(\\
&\big[M_1^{(2)}-M_0^{(2)},\,M_2^{(2)}-M_0^{(2)},\,
M_3^{(2)}-M_0^{(2)}\big]\Big),
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:supp_fusion}
\end{equation}
with a dense loss weight of $1.0$. Because the convolution is zero-initialized,
$M_{\mathrm{fus}}=M_0^{(2)}$ at initialization. Every Stage-1 head receives
deep supervision with weight $0.3$, averaged over the four heads. The total
loss is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}={}&\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{dense}}
+0.3\,\frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=0}^{3}\ell_{\mathrm{dense}}(M_k^{(1)},y)\\
&+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{top}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{role}}
+\ell_{\mathrm{dense}}(M_{\mathrm{fus}},y).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:supp_total}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Deployment Checkpoint}
\label{sec:supp_deployment}

Training uses \texttt{head\_select=gating}. The deployed checkpoint is derived
after training by setting \texttt{head\_select=fixed},
\texttt{deployment\_head=3}, and \texttt{head\_fusion=0}, and by removing the
\texttt{head\_fusion.*} tensors from the state dict so that strict loading
succeeds. Model weights, the split hash, the best epoch, and the training
history are preserved; no weight is retrained. At inference, gating still
computes the order $s$, but the Stage-2 mask in slot~3 is returned:
\begin{equation}
\hat y=\mathbb{1}\!\left[\sigma\!\left(M_3^{(2)}\right)>0.5\right],
\end{equation}
where slot~3 is the last position in the gating order. The prediction is
resized to the ground-truth resolution. The slot is selected on the
validation split and then frozen for all test datasets and seeds.
