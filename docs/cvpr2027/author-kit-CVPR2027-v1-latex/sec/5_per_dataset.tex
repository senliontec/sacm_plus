\subsection{Generalization to Unseen and Novel Datasets}
\label{sec:per_dataset}

Tables~\ref{tab:per_dataset_unseen_i}--\ref{tab:per_dataset_novel} report the
per-dataset results; best results are red bold and second-best results are
underlined.  The macro ranking in Table~\ref{tab:dcd_main} is not reproduced
on every dataset.  On Unseen data, DCD is best on Dice for CHCrack5K,
OCTA-500, and XCAD, and best on clDice for six of the eight datasets; the
clDice gains on DeepCrack, HRF, and LES-AV indicate better connectivity even
where Dice is not best.  The largest Dice margins over SACM are on OCTA-500
(+7.16) and FIVES (+2.64), while CHCrack5K (+2.16) and DeepCrack (+2.11)
also improve; XCAD is nearly tied (+0.35).  FIVES and LES-AV place DCD second
behind SAM-OCTA, and on HRF and STARE it trails the best baseline,
consistent with the difficulty of thin vessels.  DeepCrack and XCAD expose a
Dice--HD95 trade-off: DCD is second on Dice on DeepCrack but fourth on HD95,
and it leads XCAD on overlap while trailing SAM-OCTA, SegDINO, SAM-Med2D, and
SACM on HD95.  On Novel data, DCD is best on Dice, IoU, and clDice for ROAD, WIRE,
and TYRE.  TopoMortar is the main exception: DCD improves Dice over SACM
from 6.97 to 14.85 but remains behind SAM-OCTA (42.18), and its HD95 (93.02)
is far above CS2-Net (37.78).  The macro average should therefore be read as
a broad trend rather than uniform superiority.
