\paragraph{Training data.}
We follow the same 3-shot protocol as SACM~\cite{zhu2026sacm}.  From each of
six Seen curvilinear benchmarks---DRIVE~\cite{staal2004drive},
CHASEDB1~\cite{owen2009chasedb1}, DCA1~\cite{cervantes2019dca1},
CrackTree200~\cite{zou2012cracktree}, CREMI\footnote{Challenge website: \url{https://cremi.org/}.}, and
CORN~\cite{mou2019csnet}---we randomly sample three images for training and
hold out one image for validation.  This yields 18 training and 6 validation
images in total.  The support set is resampled independently for each of the
four training seeds, while all test sets are fixed across seeds.  DCD is
trained for 200 epochs, and the checkpoint with the best validation Dice is
used.  The six
datasets cover retinal vessels, coronary vessels, cracks, neuron boundaries,
and corneal nerves, spanning fundus, X-ray, EM, and microscopy images.

\paragraph{Evaluation data.}
We evaluate on 12 datasets that are never used for training or model
selection.  The eight Unseen datasets---CHCrack5K~\cite{chen2025hacnetv2},
DeepCrack~\cite{liu2019deepcrack}, FIVES~\cite{jin2022fives},
HRF~\cite{budai2013robust}, LES-AV~\cite{orlando2018glaucoma},
OCTA-500~\cite{li2024octa500}, STARE~\cite{hoover2000stare}, and
XCAD~\cite{ma2021ssvs}---contain the same structure classes as the training
data (vessels or cracks) but differ in source dataset, modality, and
acquisition conditions.  The four Novel
datasets---ROAD~\cite{mnih2013machine},
TopoMortar~\cite{valverde2025topomortar}, WIRE~\cite{zhu2026sacm},
and TYRE~\cite{zhu2026sacm}---contain structure classes that never appear during
training (roads, mortar joints, wires, and tire treads).
Table~\ref{tab:datasets} summarizes the modalities, targets, and sample
counts.  Images range from $300\times300$ (DCA1) to $3504\times2336$ (HRF)
pixels.  DCD resizes inputs to $1024\times1024$ for training and inference,
and predictions are resized back to the ground-truth resolution for
evaluation; each baseline uses the input resolution of its official recipe.
All baseline numbers are our re-runs under this protocol, using each method's
official implementation adapted to the 3-shot setting, with hyperparameters
selected on the Seen validation split; no numbers are copied from prior
papers.  Empty-ground-truth images are removed from CHCrack5K, and
CREMI uses 2D membrane boundaries derived from the raw neuron IDs rather
than the official 3D leaderboard.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
We report Dice, IoU, clDice~\cite{shit2021cldice}, and the 95th-percentile
Hausdorff distance (HD95).  clDice measures overlap between predicted and
ground-truth skeletons and reflects connectivity, whereas HD95 measures
boundary error in pixels and is sensitive to missing branches.  For each
dataset, we average the results over the four seeds and then take a
dataset-equal macro average over the 12 test datasets, so that large test
sets do not dominate.  No
method, including any baseline, is tuned on Unseen or Novel data.
