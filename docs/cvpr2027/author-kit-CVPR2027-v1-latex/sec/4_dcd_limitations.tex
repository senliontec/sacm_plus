\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our study has several limitations.  It covers 12 datasets, 18 training
images, and a single SAM ViT-L backbone; other backbones and larger support
sets remain untested.  The macro average weights datasets equally, but DCD
is not best on every dataset: it trails specialized or prompt-based methods
on HRF and STARE, and its HD95 is higher on DeepCrack, STARE, XCAD, and
TYRE.  TopoMortar remains a clear failure case relative to SAM-OCTA.  The
deployed slot is selected on a six-image validation set and then frozen;
this makes deployment deterministic but may be suboptimal for an individual
unseen dataset.  Finally, baselines follow their official recipes, whose
input resolutions and augmentation differ from DCD's; we did not re-tune
them for the 3-shot protocol, so the comparison reflects faithful ports
rather than exhaustive per-baseline tuning.  Four seeds reduce, but do not
eliminate, split variance.
