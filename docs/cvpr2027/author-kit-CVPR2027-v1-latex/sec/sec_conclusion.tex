\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

DCD revisits how a multi-candidate prompt-free decoder should deploy its
candidates.  Instead of learning a per-image selector, it trains four
candidates with dense, topology-aware, and role-specific supervision and
deploys a validation-selected fixed slot (slot~3 in our benchmark).  On a 12-dataset 3-shot
benchmark with ten baselines and four seeds, DCD ranks first on all four
macro metrics.  The ablation attributes the largest single gain to the
deployment rule and a further gain to role specialization, while showing
that fusion and topology supervision mainly affect specific metrics.  The
results also expose remaining gaps on thin-vessel and mortar datasets, where
overlap and boundary accuracy trade off.  We hope the deterministic
deployment view encourages simpler inference rules for prompt-free
segmentation.
